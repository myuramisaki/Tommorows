THE WARDEN

You choose to stay. You seal the doors from within. The loop closes. The outside world is safe. But you remain here, day after day, the same hours repeating. You have become the guardian and the prisoner both. It does not hunger anymore. It waits. And it whispers your name through the silence.

THE BREAKER

You shatter the mechanism. The timeline unravels. You wake up in your bed, sunlight through the window, the date correct. But the marks on your skin remain. And sometimes when you look in the mirror your eyes are not quite your own. You are free. But something crossed over when the wall fell. And it is learning your habits.

THE SUCCESSOR

You accept the burden. The hunger transfers. The doors open. You step out into the world, changed. Every night you feel the pull. Every dawn you know another is trapped somewhere else. You can walk away. But you know you will return. Someone has to tend the threshold. And now it is your turn.
